# Glossary

Words used in this manual and in the addon, in alphabetical order.

**Short form.** A shorter spelling of a command: `/au lang` for `/au language`, `/au rel` for `/au relationships`. Command names are always English and have no other aliases. See [[Commands Reference|Commands]].

**Archetype.** A ready-made personality, such as *Disillusioned veteran* or *Scholar*, that sets several traits at once. See [[Character Soul and Total RP 3|Character-Soul-and-Total-RP-3]].

**Automatic mode.** The only chat mode in which some lines go out without a key press: emotes, and `/say` inside instances. Off by default; it needs a confirmation. See [[Chat Gateway]].

**Axis.** One dimension of a relationship: familiarity, trust, respect, affection, fear, loyalty, and the optional attraction. See [[Relationships]].

**Feeling.** How you feel about a person, in words (trust, liking, wariness, fear, resentment, gratitude), chosen by you. See [[Relationships]].

**Observed.** What the addon counted about a player in your list (meetings, kinds, places, times), kept apart from what you chose. See [[Relationships]].

**Chance.** The probability that a moment produces a line, set per event with `/au chance`. See [[Intensity and Prose Depth|Intensity-and-Depth]].

**Channel.** Where a proposal is sent: `/say`, `/emote`, party, raid, guild or instance. *Automatic* picks `/say` or `/emote` for you.

**Character voice.** Words your character could say out loud. One of the [[four voices|The-Narrator]]; it can become a chat proposal.

**Character Soul.** Your character's personality as the addon understands it.

**Chat Gateway.** The part of the addon that decides what may reach chat, and how. See [[Chat Gateway]].

**Consent.** A switch you turn on yourself before the addon does something that touches other people or public chat, such as reading other players' Total RP 3 profiles or automatic sending. All consents are off by default.

**Cooldown.** The minimum pause between two spontaneous lines (45 seconds by default).

**Depth (prose depth).** The style of the lines: Natural, Literary or Elevated. See [[Intensity and Prose Depth|Intensity-and-Depth]].

**Disposition.** The overall warmth of a relationship, from *Hated* to *Deep bond*.

**Encounter.** A boss fight. The narrator stays almost silent during one.

**Event.** A moment the addon reacts to, such as `zone`, `battle`, `levelup` or `death`.

**Flag.** A small named marker in the journal, set by stories, dialogues or by you with `/au journal flag`.

**Inner voice.** Your character's private thoughts. One of the four voices; it never goes to chat.

**Intensity.** How often the narrator speaks, from 0 to 100 (default 50).

**Interlocutor.** The person in front of you: your target, or the NPC whose window you opened. See [[Interlocutor and Place|Interlocutor-and-Place]].

**Journal.** The window with your character's memories, places, flags, stories and relationships. See [[Memory and Journal|Memory-and-Journal]].

**Label.** What a person is to you in a relationship: stranger, acquaintance, friend, comrade in arms, enemy...

**Line.** One of the hand-written texts the narrator shows. Each situation has several versions, so lines do not repeat back to back.

**Living Memory.** The feature that remembers significant moments and recalls them honestly.

**Module.** A big switch for a whole feature, in the settings panel. See [[Settings Reference|Settings]].

**Narrator.** The private box where lines appear, and also the storyteller voice among the four voices. See [[The Narrator and the Four Voices|The-Narrator]].

**NPC Alive.** The feature that offers simulated ambient dialogues with NPCs. See [[NPC Dialogues|NPC-Dialogues]].

**NPC voice.** Something said by a person around you. One of the four voices; it never goes to chat.

**Pause.** An immediate silence of the narrator, for the session: `/au pause` or the pause key.

**Permanent memory.** A memory you pinned: it is never dropped from the journal.

**Phrase pack.** A set of lines and related content. Built-in packs come with the addon; extra packs are separate folders. See [[Phrase Packs|Phrase-Packs]].

**Preset.** A ready-made combination of modules: Complete, Personal RPG, Narrative world, Social RP.

**Proposal.** A line waiting for you to send, edit or discard it. Proposals wait 25 seconds.

**Speak button / speak key.** The button (book icon) and key that send the waiting proposal, or ask for a new one.

**Story Arc.** A small personal story of your character, with choices. See [[Story Arcs|Story-Arcs]].

**Topic to avoid.** A subject your character never touches, such as death or family. Lines and stories about it never appear.

**Total RP 3.** A separate, popular roleplay profile addon. Optional. See [[Character Soul and Total RP 3|Character-Soul-and-Total-RP-3]].

**Trait.** One part of a personality: temperament, morality, curiosity, faith, motivation, irony, inner voice.

**Validator.** The checker run by `/au validate`, which looks for mistakes in phrase packs.

**Wizard.** The setup guide that opens the first time you play. See [[First-Run Wizard|First-Run-Wizard]].
