# Azeroth Unspoken

*An immersive roleplay companion for World of Warcraft*

Welcome to the player's manual for **Azeroth Unspoken**, a roleplay addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

Azeroth Unspoken is a quiet narrator that walks beside your character. It notices where you are and what happens to you, such as a new zone, a hard fight, a serious wound, a level gained or a greeting. Now and then it shows you a short, hand-written line that fits the moment. Some lines are your character's thoughts, some are a narrator's voice, and some are words your character could say out loud, if you choose to.

> **Before you start.** Azeroth Unspoken is young (version 0.1.0). So far it has only been tested outside the game, against a simulated client, and not yet in the real WoW Forever client. Most things should just work, but some details may look or behave a little differently in game. If you find something odd, please [[tell us|Reporting-Bugs-and-Feedback]].

## What it does

- **A private narrator.** Lines appear in a small box on your screen that only you can see. You decide how often it speaks and in what style.
- **Hand-written lines, never generated.** Over a thousand situations, each with several versions in English and Italian, written by people. The same line is never repeated back to back.
- **Four voices.** The narrator, your character's inner voice, your character speaking, and the NPCs around you. See [[The Narrator and the Four Voices|The-Narrator]].
- **Your character's personality.** Give your character a temperament, a faith, a motivation or a ready-made archetype, and the lines follow it. It can also start from your [Total RP 3](https://totalrp3.info/) profile if you allow it.
- **Memory and a journal.** It remembers the moments that matter (your first time in a zone, a return, a death, a level) and recalls them only when they really happened.
- **Relationships.** It keeps track of how you stand with the NPCs you deal with, and with the players you choose to add.
- **Who you meet and where you are.** Lines change with the person in front of you and the place: a capital, a dungeon, a raid, a battleground.
- **NPC ambient dialogues.** A small, clearly labelled, simulated chat with innkeepers, merchants, guards, flight masters and trainers.
- **Personal stories.** Small story arcs for your character that grow from real events, with choices that are yours.
- **Optional chat lines.** If you want, your character's lines can become proposals that you edit and send to chat with one key.

## What it never does

- **Nothing reaches public chat without your action.** With the default settings, nothing ever leaves the narrator box. Even when you turn chat proposals on, a line goes out only when you press a key, click a button or type a command. The single exception is the *automatic* mode, which you must choose yourself and confirm twice.
- **It is fully offline.** No internet connection, no external service, no artificial intelligence, no tracking. Everything stays on your computer.
- **It does not save chat.** It never stores the text of chat messages or whispers.
- **It does not change the game.** No quests, rewards, vendors or game windows are touched. The stories and dialogues are the addon's own storytelling.

## Quick start

1. **Install it.** Copy the `AzerothUnspoken` folder into `<WoW>\_forever_\Interface\AddOns`. See [[Installation and Updating|Installation-and-Updating]].
2. **Log in.** A few seconds after you enter the world, the [[setup wizard|First-Run-Wizard]] opens. Pick your language first (English or Italiano): the addon starts in English. Then pick a style and an intensity. You can skip any step.
3. **Play.** Walk into a new zone, start a fight, level up. Lines appear in the narrator box near the bottom of the screen.
4. **Try a few commands.** Type `/au` for the list, `/au pause` to silence the narrator at once, and `/au options` for the settings. See the [[Commands Reference|Commands]].
5. **Make it yours.** Give your character a personality with `/au soul`, and read your journal with `/au journal`.
