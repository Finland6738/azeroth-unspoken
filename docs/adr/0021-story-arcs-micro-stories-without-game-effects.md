# 0021. Story Arcs: micro-stories that do not touch the game

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-29 (step S9; GDD §5.4, §7.4, §7.5, §10, §12.2; ADR 0003, 0005, 0015, 0016, 0017, 0018, 0019, 0020)

## Context

The GDD (§7.5) calls for "personal micro-stories, not server quests": an arc with states (`LOCKED`, `DISCOVERED`, `REFLECTED`, `RESOLVED`), which opens with a real event or by the player's choice, advances only from accessible events and closes with a persistent memory. No reward, no change to real NPCs, no false promise of consequences on the server, at most 1–2 active arcs, scenes that can be skipped and disabled.

There are three risks. An arc that opens "by itself" without a real event attributes to the character a story that does not belong to them. An arc that talks too much, or at every step together with the others, becomes a cascade of messages (§10). And an arc that asks the player "what do you feel?" and then writes it in their place takes away from the player the control over who the character is (§5.4, ADR 0016).

## Decision

1. **Arcs are data, not code.** `NS:RegisterArcs{ pack, arcs, texts }` registers the structure (`Data/Arcs.lua`): states, steps, choices. The lines are rules of three events (`arc_open`, `arc_step`, `arc_resolve`, in `Data/RegistryArcs.lua`) with the condition `arc = { id, state, choice }`; the journal and choice texts are per language (`NS:AddArcTexts`). A pack adds arcs without touching the engine ([pack guide](../../AzerothUnspoken/PACK_FORMAT.md), "Story Arcs").
2. **Base states and custom states.** `LOCKED → DISCOVERED → REFLECTED → RESOLVED`; an arc can declare its own states (`capitals` has `JOURNEY`), but from a state one goes **only to the next**: no jumps, no steps back. The validator checks the definition (`SA:Validate`); a wrong arc stays registered and **never starts**.
3. **Only real events.** A step has `on = { … }` among six events: `zone` (place by `mapID` and name, after two seconds), `death`, `level`, `npc` (gossip, merchant, flight, trainer, innkeeper, bank: not quests), `flag` (a new narrative flag set by an addon system, for example an ambient dialogue; not those set by hand with `/au journal flag` nor the automatic memory ones, which are already inside the event that causes them: [ADR 0020](0020-simulated-dialogue-and-real-dialogue-npc-alive.md)), `player` (a direct choice by the player). The `when` clauses are RuleEngine conditions evaluated on the context of the moment, so memory, relationships, places and interlocutor are the same as for everything else. The opening cannot be a `player` step and cannot ask for a choice. Neither a command, nor a timer, nor another system opens or advances an arc: `SA:Fire` is subscribed to the bus (and `SA:Choose`/`Start` require an arc that is already visible). An event moves an arc by **one step only**: the `arc_<id>_<state>` flags that the arcs themselves leave do not trigger others.
4. **The player chooses, the addon does not write for them.** A step with `choose` waits for the player among two or three options (journal, `/au stories choose`); the choice is saved as an **option id**, never as text. The lines after the choice (`arc.choice`) follow what the player decided; before a choice no line attributes a feeling to the character. The arc voices are private (`inner`, `narrator`): never `character`, which can become a chat proposal ([ADR 0003](0003-private-narrator-and-chat-gateway.md)). An arc on a sensitive topic (`topic = "death"`) does not open or advance if the character avoids it ([ADR 0016](0016-character-soul-opt-in-traits-and-total-rp-3-with-consent.md)).
5. **1–2 active arcs, skip, suspend, disable.** `arcs.maxActive` (1–2, default 2) counts the active arcs (open, not concluded, in `active` mode). When the slots are full the opening event does not open the arc: it leaves it "available" (`waiting`) and the player starts it from the journal or with `/au stories start`. Each arc can be suspended (it stays as it is, frees a slot), skipped (it does not open or advance; it can be resumed) or disabled (even before it opens), individually or all at once; the `storyArcs` module switches everything off. A concluded arc is no longer touched.
6. **Closing with a memory.** `RESOLVED` records in Living Memory a **permanent** `story` memory, with only place and tags (`arc`, `done`, the id): no text, no name. Each state sets the flag `arc_<id>_<state>`, which lines and dialogues read with `remembered`.
7. **The Narrative Director manages the voice.** The `arc_*` events have "exceptional event" priority and category `reflection`; they go through `Director:Offer`, which now returns the outcome to the proposer (`onResult`). `SA.owed` holds the lines that could not speak: **only one at a time**, at least `BEAT_GAP` (90 s) between two arc lines, never in combat, when dead, in a raid or battleground, or over an intrusive screen; during a pause they wait, with intensity at 0 or the module off they lapse; after 15 minutes they lapse anyway. **The state advances even in silence**: the journal tells every state, and silence is a valid outcome (GDD §6).
8. **The journal** (`UI/JournalFrame.lua`, "Stories" tab) shows the arcs in progress, those that can begin, the concluded ones, and the skipped or disabled ones, with the account of every state passed through and **the choices made**; from there the options are chosen and arcs are suspended, skipped or disabled. A discreet notice in the narrator (not in chat) says that an arc is waiting for a choice or can begin.
9. **No reward and no effect on the game.** An arc definition has no fields for rewards, quests, items or NPCs: an unknown field is a validator error. Arcs **read** the relationships (S6b) and the NPC registry (S6c, S8), they do not write them. The texts avoid promises ("mission", "loot") and a test checks this.
10. **Versioned persistence.** State in `AzerothUnspokenCharacterDB.arcs = { version, items, stats }`; **schema 6** with a character migration (the empty placeholder becomes the current shape; a corrupted value restarts from zero) and an internal migration (`SA.migrations`, `arcs.version`). `SA:Sanitize` discards malformed entries, brings a state the arc does not have back to `LOCKED`, keeps the entries of unregistered arcs and leaves data from a newer version **read-only**. A pending choice, the mode (suspended, skipped, disabled) and the choices made survive a `/reload`; pending lines do not (a line is never repeated after a load).

## Alternatives considered

- **Opening arcs by the player's free choice at any moment**: rejected. It would lose the sense of the GDD ("opening with first visit…"): the arc is the story of something that happened. The player chooses *how* and *whether* to continue, not *what* has happened to them.
- **A Director event for each state of each arc** (`arc_path_reflected`…): rejected. Twenty events to declare, probabilities and pauses to tune one by one. Three events with the `arc` condition are enough and the guide stays short.
- **A queue of arcs that open by themselves when a slot frees up**: rejected. An arc that starts while no one is looking is exactly the surprise the player did not ask for; it stays "available" and the player decides.
- **Saving the text of the choice or of the line**: rejected. The text would change with the language and is not needed: the option id and the journal account (arc data) are enough.
- **A line for every step, always**: rejected. With arcs active together there would be a cascade; with `BEAT_GAP` and the queue the voice is only one and the rest is in the journal.

*Step 0 update ([ADR 0023](0023-english-first-addon-and-english-command-names.md)):* the commands named here are written with their English names, `/au journal flag` and `/au stories`; the Italian names this ADR first used were removed, and an Italian word now only gets a hint to the English command.

## Consequences

- Positive: the character lives small stories without the addon promising anything to the game; the player remains master of what the character feels; the addon is silent when it matters; arcs are data and a pack adds more; every illegal transition is rejected and tested.
- Negative or costs: the thresholds (familiarity 25/40/55, levels, waits) and the place numbers are hypotheses not tested in game; arcs depend on data the client might not provide (`C_Map` for the `uiMapID`s, the English zone name as a fallback); the `npcID`s and `uiMapID`s of `Data/NPCs.lua` and `Data/Places.lua` remain to be verified ([#36](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-unspoken/issues/36)). The journal has only text rows and narrow buttons: the reading of long accounts is broken by hand over several rows.
