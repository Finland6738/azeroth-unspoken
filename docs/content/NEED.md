# Content need

Generated by `python tools/archive/cli.py need --write` from the model `tools/archive/need_model.json` ([ADR 0050](../adr/0050-need-model-near-duplicates-and-content-waves.md)); do not edit by hand. The machine-readable report is [need.json](need.json); the plan of the waves is [WAVES.md](WAVES.md).

**Need** = how many distinct texts per language a pool must hold so that no character hears one of its texts twice in **400 hours** of play (strict) or in any **40 hours** in a row (soft), at the default pace of **22.5** interventions per hour and the default depth `literary`. It assumes an anti-repetition that exhausts a pool before repeating (see the ADR: today's engine keeps a window of 30 picks per event). Counted state: `approved`.

- With **today's pick** (random among the lines of the event not in its window of 30 picks; ADR 0050) the same pools would need about **132,900** texts per language for at most 10% of the interventions of 400 hours to be repeats, **275,500** for at most 5%: the owner's range of 100,000-300,000 (ADR 0024). **Owner decision (2026-11-02):** the pick stays random, with no exhaustive memory of what was said, and the waves go on past the strict need up to the **random need** above (at most 5% repeats), spread over the pools like the strict one.
- The firings of an event are spread over its levels of specificity as the engine mixes them (owner decision of 2026-11-04, [ADR 0051](../adr/0051-tiers-mixed-by-share.md)): place 50%, situation 35%, generic 15%, renormalised over the levels the event plans; the situation layers share theirs by the weights of the model.
- The rates of `heal`, `buff` and `rezother` change with the class ([ADR 0054](../adr/0054-event-rates-per-class-and-the-gestures-a-class-makes-for-itself.md)): they fire only on the character's own casts, so a class pool fires at its class's factor (`class_rates` of the model), a pool the class cannot fire has no need, and every other pool fires for the class of the race that fires it most.
- A heal or a buff cast on oneself and one cast on someone else are two halves of a pool ([ADR 0055](../adr/0055-a-cast-on-oneself-and-a-cast-on-someone-else.md)): `<event>:self` and `<event>:other`, by the share of each class's casts on itself (`cast_on_self` of the model). Their lines write `castOnSelf = true` or `false`; a line without the condition counts in both halves by their firings. A pool never cast on someone else keeps the plain event.
- The need grows in proportion to the pace: at intensity 100 the spontaneous budgets double.
- Texts beyond the strict need of their pool (they still widen the choice): enUS 3,429, itIT 3,429.
- Waves planned: **89** of about 2,500 texts per language ([WAVES.md](WAVES.md)).
- Event mix measured by `python -I -X utf8 Tests/bench_trial.py --mix 20 --seeds 1,2 --model` (91.89 hours in the open world, 28.15 in a capital; 36.587 and 28.315 interventions per hour before the scaling).
