# Third-party material and licences

Azeroth Unspoken has a mixed licence: the code is GPL-3.0-or-later, the phrases and narrative data are all rights reserved,
the example pack is MIT, the documents are CC BY 4.0 ([LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md)). This page records where every
font and picture that ships in the addon folder comes from and under which licence. It is the single place to update when
one is added (UI-ECO step UI-1, [ADR 0058](adr/0058-nebula-ecosystem-look-and-credits.md)). The files are in
`AzerothUnspoken/Media/`; the table of them is `AzerothUnspoken/UI/Assets.lua`; `Tests/check_media.py` fails when a file is
missing from the table, when a path of the table has no file, or when a font family has no licence text.

## Fonts (`AzerothUnspoken/Media/Fonts/`)

The fonts are the same files that Finland Dragon Credit Points (FDCP), the sister addon, ships, under the same licences.

| Family | Files | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Outfit** (The Outfit Project Authors) | `Outfit-Regular.ttf`, `Outfit-Medium.ttf`, `Outfit-SemiBold.ttf` | SIL Open Font License 1.1, text in `Outfit-OFL.txt` | Static instances cut from the variable font and reduced to the Latin subset by `tools/make_fonts.py`. No Reserved Font Name. |
| **JetBrains Mono** (The JetBrains Mono Project Authors) | `JetBrainsMono-Regular.ttf`, `JetBrainsMono-Medium.ttf` | SIL Open Font License 1.1, text in `JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt` | Same treatment. No Reserved Font Name. |
| **Orbitron** (Matt McInerney, The League of Moveable Type) | `Orbitron-Bold.ttf` | SIL Open Font License 1.1, text in `Orbitron-OFL.txt` | Declares the Reserved Font Name "Orbitron", so the file ships **whole and unmodified**. Meant for the window title only, in capitals: it has no middle dot and no arrows. |

The OFL allows bundling the fonts in software under any licence provided the fonts are not sold on their own, the licence
text accompanies them and a modified version does not reuse a Reserved Font Name. The licence texts are copied as they
are (the copyright line of `Orbitron-OFL.txt` names its author's published contact address; `Tests/check_privacy.py` allows exactly that file and address). The logo font of the NEBULA community site (Atomed) has no licence that allows redistribution and is **not**
included. The addon falls back to the game's own fonts when a file cannot be loaded, and a setting will let the player
choose them (step UI-3). Nothing uses the fonts yet: the widgets that do arrive in steps UI-2 and UI-3.

## Icons (`Media/icons.tga`)

The 44 icons of the sheet are **drawn for this addon** by `tools/make_media.py` from simple Pillow shapes (lines, arcs,
ellipses, polygons): the first 40 are those of FDCP (also drawn by its own tool), the last four (heart, quill, chat,
scroll) were drawn for this addon. No third-party artwork is copied or traced; they are covered by the addon's code licence,
GPL-3.0-or-later. The names and the order of the cells are in `UI/Assets.lua` (`Assets.icons`) and in `ICON_NAMES` of the
tool, and a test keeps the two equal.

## Textures (`Media/*.tga`)

The rounded rectangles and rings, the shadow, the two glows, the pill, the circle and the radial fall-off are generated by
`tools/make_media.py` (white shapes that the theme tints at run time). Nothing in them comes from outside the repository;
they are covered by GPL-3.0-or-later like the tool that makes them.

## The addon's own logo (`Media/logo_256.tga`, `Media/logo_64.tga`)

Made by `tools/make_media.py` from `assets/logo-source.webp`, the addon's own logo (an open scroll with a green wisp rising
from it), supplied by the repository owner, cut to a circle with an anti-aliased edge. It is a picture of the project and
not part of the code: the GPL-3.0 of the code does not by itself grant a right to use the logo to present another project.

## The NEBULA community logo (`Media/nebula_dark_256.tga`, `Media/nebula_light_256.tga`)

The logo of the NEBULA community, in two variants supplied by the repository owner: the dark-theme art (an orange, pink and
purple swirl with white stars and the word NEBULA in white, shown with the Nebula theme) and the light-theme art (a blue and
green swirl with black stars and word, shown with the Aurora theme). The sources are `assets/nebula/nebula-logo-dark.png` and
`assets/nebula/nebula-logo-light.png` (the owner's 1024 by 1024 files, cropped to their content, 512 by 492); `tools/make_media.py`
centres each on a transparent square and scales it to 256 by 256. They are full colour and are never tinted. The Settings > Info
page shows the one of the active theme (`UI/SettingsFrame.lua`); the README, the wiki About page and the CurseForge text show both
with `prefers-color-scheme`.

**Licence.** The logo is used with its owner's permission. It is a picture of the NEBULA community, **not** part of the code:
the GPL-3.0-or-later of the code does not grant a right to use it, and it is covered by no licence of this repository. A
copy of the repository that is shared again must remove it or ask its owner. Everything else in this page is original or
under a licence that allows bundling.

## Mockups

The mockups of `docs/design/mockups/`, `docs/specs/mockups/` and `assets/` are made by `tools/mockup_lib.py` and
`tools/make_mockups.py`, adapted from FDCP's tools of the same name (same owner, GPL-3.0-or-later). They load the fonts
above from `AzerothUnspoken/Media/Fonts/` and show the addon's own logo and the NEBULA community logo (under the terms
just above); the landscapes are SVG drawings of `docs/design/mockups/tools/mockkit.py`, the icons line drawings of
`mockup_lib.py`. The side-by-side page of `docs/specs/mockups/` links FDCP's own pictures from its repository; none is
copied here.
