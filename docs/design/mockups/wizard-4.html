Style
How elevated should the language be? It changes which lines the narrator prefers: the others stay possible, but less likely.
Natural
Everyday and concise. "These walls have stood for centuries. I wonder who lived here."
Literary (default)
Evocative but readable. "The stones still keep the traces of those who called this place home."
Elevated
Solemn and ceremonial. "The towers fell and the voices went quiet, yet the stones still keep watch over the lost days."
