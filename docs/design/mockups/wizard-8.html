Keys, button and settings
No key is bound until you choose. Bind these under Options > Keybindings > AddOns:
Speak: asks for a line, and sends the proposal when there is one (never in private mode).
Pause: silences the narrator at once; press again to resume.
Another line: swaps the proposal for another one.
NPC: opens or closes the ambient dialogue with the NPC in front of you.
The speak button sits beside the narrator box (default: on); drag it with the right mouse button, /au button shows or hides it. Settings: /au options. All commands: /au help. This guide again: /au wizard.
Defaults: intensity 50, 45 s between lines, a proposal waits 25 s, chat: private narrator only, speak button: on, Total RP 3 of others: off, proximity: off, shared list: off.
