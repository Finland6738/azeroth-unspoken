A walking song comes back, the kind that keeps the feet in time:
The hill is steep, the pack is old,
the strap has worn a groove;
but feet that know the way they go
will find the way to move.
 
The dust will rise, the dust will fall,
the boots will shake it free;
the road remembers every step,
then lets the walker be.
 
So step and step, the road is long,
and shorter for a song.
