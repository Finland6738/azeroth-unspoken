What may reach chat?
Private by default: the narrator's lines stay on your screen. Whatever you pick, every send in your character's name starts from a gesture of yours, except in automatic.
private narrator only (default)
Lines stay on your screen. Nothing ever goes to chat.
preview
The speak key opens the line in an editor; you send it with Enter.
confirm to send
The speak key or button sends the proposed line in one gesture.
automatic (where allowed)
Emotes, and /say in instances, are posted by themselves in your character's name, visible to everyone nearby. Needs a separate confirmation.
