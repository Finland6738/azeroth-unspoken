What may reach chat? By default nothing: the narrator stays private. Whatever you pick, every send in your character's name starts only from a gesture of yours, except in 'automatic'.
» private narrator only «
Private narrator only (default): lines appear in the box and stay visible to you alone. Nothing goes to chat.
preview
Preview: the character's lines become proposals. The 'speak' key opens the editor, where you can change them and send with Enter.
confirm to send
Confirm to send: the proposal is ready and the 'speak' key or button sends it in one gesture. You can always edit or discard it.
automatic (where allowed)
Automatic where allowed: emotes go out by themselves, and /say in instances; the rest stays a proposal. A few per minute, always. It turns on only with an explicit confirmation.
